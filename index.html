About Me

I am Yagavan T M, an undergraduate student currently in my second year studying Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology in Coimbatore.

My interest lies at the intersection of robust backend engineering, structured database systems, and practical machine learning applications. SMART ERM – Smart Emergency Room Manager

Educational simulation demonstrating algorithmic priority queue scheduling; not a clinical diagnostic system.
